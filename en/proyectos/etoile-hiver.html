EVENTS · DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

Étoile d’Hiver · Hybrid event

Sensory launch of a perfume that connects an in-person gala, interactive streaming and boxes sent to the online public.

Academic proposal · fictitious brand

The challenge

Transfer an olfactory experience to the digital environment and coordinate production, communication, logistics and commercial monitoring.

Project contents

  • Noël Étoilé concept · The Night of Aroma, for the fictitious brand Maison Aurelia.
  • In-person and online program, simultaneous opening of sensory boxes and audiovisual launch piece.
  • Streaming with chat, social networks, QR codes, CRM and communication plan before, during and after.
  • Team, protocol, an estimated budget of €51,450, performance indicators and contingency plans.

Scope and status

It is a proposed event plan for November 2026. Capacity, registrations, spectators and sales are forecasts or objectives, not figures of an executed event.

Applied skills

Hybrid production · protocol · logistics · communication · budget
