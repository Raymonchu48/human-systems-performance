NUTRITION · HEALTHY HABITS

Nutrition · Healthy habits

Analysis of two cases with nutritional assessment, dietary proposals, nutritional education and a progressive physical activity plan.

Academic work case analysis

The goal

Study how to adapt food, nutritional education and physical activity to the needs and context of two people at different stages of life.

What the project covers

  • Assessment of the nutritional situation and eating habits in the cases of Manuel and Claudia.
  • Proposals for dietary changes and examples of daily menus adapted to each case.
  • Nutritional education strategies and involvement of the family environment.
  • Progressive physical activity plan organized into activation, consolidation and autonomy phases.

Scope and status

Academic analysis and proposal exercise. The document presents guidelines and expected results, without accrediting interventions carried out or clinical results observed.

Applied skills

Nutritional analysis · food education · menu planning · change of habits · adapted physical activity
