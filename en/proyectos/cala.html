UX/UI · INCLUSIVE DESIGN

Cala · Usability and accessibility

Evaluation of a vacation rental platform: understanding the journey, price transparency and barriers to entry.

Academic project prototype inspection

The challenge

Facilitate the search and reservation of accommodation for people with different interaction needs, without losing clarity in prices and conditions.

Project contents

  • Four-screen HTML, CSS, and JavaScript inspection: explore, detail, reserve, and confirmation.
  • Detection of static controls, keyboard barriers, insufficient contrast and clipping risks when zooming.
  • Inclusive protocol proposed for eight participants, tasks, indicators and observation sheet.
  • Improvement plan with WCAG 2.2 AA objective and privacy measures.

Scope and status

Code inspection has been performed. Participant sessions, browser testing, and full audit are pending. WCAG 2.2 AA is a goal, not an accredited conformance.

Applied skills

UX inspection · accessibility · inclusive design · prioritization
