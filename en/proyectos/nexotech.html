UX · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

NexoTech · UX content strategy

A proposal to improve findability, meaningful interaction and return visits across a technology content ecosystem.

Academic case · fictitious organization

The challenge

Connect blog, networks and newsletter through content that each user can find, understand and apply.

Project contents

  • Functional benchmark, interview plan, analytics, card sorting and tree testing.
  • Architecture by intention: Learn, Solve, Current Affairs and Resources, with level and format filters.
  • Empathy map, MoSCoW prioritization and impact and effort matrix.
  • Specification of a flow for Figma, usability script and measurement roadmap.

Scope and status

NexoTech is a fictitious case. The data from the card sorting pilot is simulated and improvements in CTR, interaction and recurrence are proposed goals. The document does not accredit results with users or a published prototype.

UX research · content strategy · architecture · measurement
